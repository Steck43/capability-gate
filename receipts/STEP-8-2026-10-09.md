# STEP-8 — Three-object boundary (2026-10-09)

## Scope measured

atoms issues a per-call box ticket, and `box_entry` returns a prove-shaped
receipt bound to this call (call digest + ticket digest + content hash).
Fake, replayed, and wrong-call-id receipts are denied on isolation-layer
`tests/test_box_entry_bind.py` (failing stub first at `3e83ad7`, deny rules at
`90a0835`).
