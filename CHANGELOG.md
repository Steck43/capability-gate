A policy `isolate` list gives the listed tools the verdict `isolate` once their grant and path checks pass; a tool in both `isolate` and `require_approval` is refused at load. - Required controls check (`control_check.py`, `controls/catalog.yaml`, `controls/profile.example.yaml`). It diffs the old and new allowlist against a profile of required controls and refuses a change that leaves one unmet: a granted tool dropped from `require_approval`, a new skill, tool or path glob, a granted tool taken out of `isolate`, or a grant of a tool the profile keeps ungranted. Constant folding covers literals and empty literals, names assigned exactly once (module, function, tuple unpacking, annotated, walrus, chained), ternaries, `and` and `or` with a constant side, `not`, arithmetic, comparisons and chains, membership in an empty container, subscripts and slices, and pure builtins (`bool`, `int`, `len` and the rest); a second binding of any kind, or a mutable literal passed anywhere it could change, stops folding. Skip and xfail forms: in a KAT file, any reference to `pytest.mark.skip` (decorator, class mark, `pytestmark`, `pytest.param(marks=...)`, `add_marker`, alias, `getattr`, `with_args`, wrapper) or the `unittest` skip and expected-failure decorators, any imperative skip, xfail, importorskip or `skipTest` not under an `if` that is purely a platform check, and a `skipif` whose condition is neither purely a platform check nor constant false. In every test file: `skipif` with no condition or a condition that folds true (string conditions are parsed), a condition that mixes a platform check with anything else or names a platform value that does not exist, a condition taken from a function parameter, `xfail(strict=...)` that is not provably true, `xfail(run=False)`, `**` arguments to `skipif` or `xfail`, imperative `pytest.xfail`, and in a test function a forced exit or dynamic code. Each KAT must reach the real gate on its straight-line path before any return or raise: `.evaluate` or `.throw` on a `capability_gate.Gate` built in the test or returned by a same-file fixture or helper; `open_authorized_read`, `_pre`, `main` or `pre_tool_call` imported from `capability_gate` or `adapters`; or a subprocess running `adapters/claude_code_hook.py`. A gate from a test helper module, conftest, a local or subclassed class, `getattr`, `type()` or `importlib` does not count, nor does a gate module beside the test file, a gate the test's world patches (`setattr`, `monkeypatch`, `mock.patch` or attribute assignment on a gate, the Gate class or a gate function, in the test, its fixtures, autouse fixtures, helpers or the module), or a call in dead code (constant-false branch, empty loop or comprehension, unconsumed generator, `while` body) or inside `pytest.raises`, `contextlib.suppress` or `assertRaises`. Each KAT must then assert on the gate's result, directly, through a name, a flag or counter set under a branch, `match` or handler that depends on it, a list it was appended to, a same-file fixture that returns it, or a same-file helper that asserts on it. Known gaps: one file at a time, so a gate patched or a fixture supplied from conftest or another module, a module loaded by file path, and environment-dependent conditions outside skip marks are not judged; fault injection into anything other than a gate object, the Gate class and the gate functions is allowed on purpose. `tests/test_kat_lint.py`.
